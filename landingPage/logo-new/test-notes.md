# Round 1 — what the tests showed

Tools run: `svg_audit.py` (all three), `concept_sheet.py` (true 16/32/64 px), `preview_sheet.py`
(scale ladder, pixel test, light/dark backgrounds, one-colour), inspected in the browser.

**Audit:** all three are 1 colour, no gradients, no live text, no rasters, no near-miss angles.
17–25 anchors each. Readiness 99/100. Optical centring corrected on all three (mark sits slightly
above geometric centre).

**Fixes made between v1 and v3**
- A v1 read as a serving cloche — the dome was too shallow and the lantern looked like a lid knob.
  Added a colonnaded drum and a taller cupola; it now reads as a rotunda.
- B v1/v2: the white wedge inside the crown pinched to a near-point (ink trap). Widened the crown top
  and evened the outline band to 12 units, so the hollow half reads as a drawn line, not a misprint.
- C v1: the two bowls met in a knife-point cusp at the waist. Rebuilt both bowls on non-vertical chords
  so the waist is a shallow joint.

**Other readings:** none of the three produce an unintended shape mirrored or rotated. A's nearest
cliché is the civic/bank rotunda; C's is "just a heavy B". B has no close neighbour I can name.
