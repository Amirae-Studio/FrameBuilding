Brand identity · v1

Out of the frame.
Onto your shelf.

The FrameBuilding mark shows what the project is: one landmark, set on a plinth you design, printed layer by layer, and rising out of its frame. It's a sibling to FrameCity, but it stands on its own.

01 · Anatomy

Every line means something.

1 2 3 4 5
  • The spire breaks the frameThe landmark rises through the top edge. The model isn't a picture on a wall: it leaves the frame and stands on your shelf.
  • The frameA square, open-cornered picture frame. It ties FrameBuilding to FrameCity's framed cities without copying them.
  • Two sharp setbacksAn Art Deco skyline profile with a dominant shaft. It reads as "landmark" at any size and points to the New York towers in the launch set.
  • Floors, printed in layersThe cuts across the shaft are both floors and the layers of a 3D print. This is a model you make, not a photo.
  • The plinthThe base you design in the Studio. It's in the frame colour, because the base is what makes each print yours.

02 · Concepts

Three directions, one pick.

All three use the same plinth and two-weight wordmark, so the family stays consistent whichever mark leads.

Recommended

A · Breakout

A landmark on a plinth, rising out of its frame. It tells the whole story (frame, building, base, print) and stays legible down to a 16px favicon.

Alternative

B · Setback F

A tower whose setbacks spell an F. It's a bold monogram and great as an app icon, but it loses the frame, which links the project to FrameCity.

Alternative

C · Portico

A classical front in viewfinder corners. It's elegant and suits heritage landmarks, but it skews historic for a library that includes skyscrapers.

03 · Lockups

On every background.

Use the horizontal lockup in headers and on wide surfaces, the stacked lockup for square formats and covers, and the mark alone once the name is already on screen.

Clear space: keep at least the width of the frame's side margin (a quarter of the mark's height) empty on every side. Minimum size: 32px tall for the horizontal lockup and 16px for the favicon tile, which drops the floor cuts so it stays crisp.
